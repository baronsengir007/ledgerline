Three steps, then it runs itself

1 /

Connect your accounts

Link your bank feed and your invoicing tool. Read-only access: Ledgerline can see transactions, never move money.

2 /

Ledgerline matches

Amounts, references, names and near-misses are compared on every new transaction. Each match carries a confidence score you can inspect.

3 /

You review the exceptions

High-confidence matches close themselves. Partial payments, bundles and unclear references land in one review queue, ordered by amount.

Built for the messy reality of getting paid

Confidence you can audit

Every automatic match shows why it matched: amount, reference, counterparty history. One click to unmatch.

Partial payments and bundles

One payment for three invoices, or half an invoice now and half next month. Ledgerline splits and links them correctly.

Multi-currency

Invoices in euros, payments in pounds. Matched at the daily rate, differences booked to an account you choose.

An audit trail that holds up

Every match, split and manual correction is logged with who, when and why. Export the trail with your books.

A digest, not a dashboard vigil

One morning summary in Slack or email: what closed itself, what needs you. No login required to know where you stand.

Leave whenever you want

Your matches export to CSV and to your accounting tool at any moment. Cancelling takes two clicks and keeps your data available for 90 days.
